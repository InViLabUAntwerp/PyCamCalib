Distortion Map

This plot visualizes the lens distortion that the calibration has measured and can now correct.

The arrows show the direction and magnitude of the distortion at different points in the image. They point from a distorted point to its corrected, ideal position.

The contour lines connect areas of equal distortion magnitude, similar to a topographic map. In a typical "barrel" distortion, the distortion is minimal at the center and increases towards the edges of the image.

The goal of undistortion is to apply the inverse of this map to an image, making all straight lines in the real world appear straight in the final image.
