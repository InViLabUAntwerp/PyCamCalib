Detections Viewer

This viewer allows you to inspect the feature detection results for each individual image.

Use the slider to navigate through all the images you uploaded.

Point Colors:

  • Green: The corners were successfully detected and were included in the final calibration calculation.
  • Red: The corners were detected, but the image was not used in the final calculation (either because it was manually excluded as an outlier, or it failed the 'absolute' check).
  • If no points are visible, it means the detector failed to find the calibration pattern in this image.
