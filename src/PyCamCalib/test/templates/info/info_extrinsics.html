3D Board Positions (Extrinsics)

This plot shows the position and orientation (the "pose") of the calibration board relative to the camera for each image.

The camera is fixed at the origin (0,0,0) of the coordinate system. Each cloud of points represents one of the calibration images, showing where the board was located in 3D space when the picture was taken.

A good set of calibration images will have the board in a wide variety of positions and angles, covering the center, corners, and edges of the camera's field of view. This ensures that the algorithm gets enough information to model the lens distortion accurately across the entire sensor.
